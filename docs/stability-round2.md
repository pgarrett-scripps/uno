# UNO backend stability experiments, round 2

All four prototypes completed their prespecified 30-run low-input screen: **120 fresh candidate searches**, each compared with a hash-verified accepted-V3 result. **None qualifies for adoption.** Broader RT calibration is promising, but introduces a new empty result and loses identification depth on particular files. Accepted V3 remains unchanged.

## What each experiment taught us

**Broader training evidence:** candidate-local q≤25% positives were retained across the existing deviation cuts, and a per-fold class-support check replaced the minimum-protein ML gate. This activated 48 additional model fits: all had zero positives in their selected deviation result, rather than 1–19. Total fits increased from 58 corresponding V3 fits to 106, yet there were no rescues. One successful result collapsed (1ng rep4, seed8: 211→0 targets); four files lost more than 5% median reported human IDs. Initial calibration traces matched in all 30 pairs. More available training labels does not guarantee useful training labels; the experiment changes both label pooling and model eligibility, so it does not isolate their individual effects. [Formal report](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/evidence-stage-a-report.md) · [Mechanism analysis](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/evidence_lowinput/mechanism.md).

**Broader RT calibration:** a feature-balanced, mass-matched pool supplied a constrained affine RT fit, with background subtraction and an uncertainty floor. The fitted map was frozen through later RT stages. Reported-count variability decreased **32.3%**, summed human IDs increased **15.5%**, and pooled FDP decreased. Three empty results were rescued: 1ng rep4 seed1 (272 targets), 200pg rep1 seed8 (208), and 200pg rep3 seed5 (410). However, 200pg rep3 seed0 collapsed from 220 targets to 0. Median reported human IDs fell 353→317 on 1ng rep4 and 403→269 on 200pg rep2; the latter also lost 6.05% median human depth at the independent entrapment 5% threshold. All38 calibration passes used broad-evidence fitting rather than an eligibility or weak-evidence fallback. These benefits therefore coexist with material regressions. [Formal report](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/rt-stage-a-report.md) · [Mechanism analysis](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/rt_lowinput/rt-mechanism-notes.md).

Of the 38 RT fits, 28 reached the prescribed slope cap and all supported exactly six of eight strata. On 200pg rep3, the new collapse and the remaining seed8 failure used uncertainty floors around 1.76–1.95 minutes, versus approximately 0.65–0.70 minutes in successful reruns. This post hoc association cannot isolate the effect of the floor: the map, freezing policy and floor changed together.

The three RT rescues together contain 888 human and 2 yeast IDs (pooled entrapment FDP 0.767%); one rescue individually exceeds 1%. Median within-file target-list Jaccard is 0.502 for V3 and 0.641 for RT across 59 defined seed pairs in each variant. These are descriptive overlap summaries; the sets of defined pairs can differ, and empty/empty is undefined. Better aggregate counts and overlap do not establish reliable behavior for every file.

**Multiple-decoy bootstrap:** three auxiliary shuffled-decoy populations estimated only the initial calibration-anchor background. The provisional criterion used pooled decoy tail counts, inclusive score ties and a +1 correction; the final reporting threshold and final original target/decoy competition stayed fixed. Every candidate returned zero IDs because the initial bootstrap selected no confident anchors. This rejects the particular combined estimator tested here; it does not establish that averaging decoy evidence itself cannot work. Candidate FDP is undefined. The formal reporter consequently labels its overall result “INCONCLUSIVE: KEEP V3,” while its new-empty, depth, human-count and improvement criteria explicitly fail. [Formal report](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/decoys-stage-a-report.md) · [Mechanism analysis](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/decoys_lowinput/mechanism.md).

A separately archived, diagnostic-only reconstruction of 1ng rep1 seed0 reproduced both the frozen prototype’s zero anchors and V3’s 19 first-round anchors. Holding the pooled scores fixed, removing the +1 term would admit five targets; the inclusive versus strict tie choice did not change that count. The minimum corrected ratio was 20%, above the 5% provisional cutoff. The pooled and original background probabilities were nearly identical (0.413900 versus 0.413918), but the pooled score-tail counts differed. This demonstrates why the intended combined estimator was too stringent in this one case; it is not a downstream validation of a modified candidate. [Diagnostic findings and provenance](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/decoys_background_diagnostic/findings.md).

**Simpler classifier:** the default Sequoia model used depth 3 instead of 7, lambda 10 instead of 1, and full row/column sampling instead of 0.8; labels and folds were retained. One empty result was rescued (200pg rep3 seed5: 155 human IDs, no yeast), but reported-count variability worsened, and four files lost more than 5% median reported human IDs. Independent entrapment-depth guards also failed. Initial calibration traces and first-pass training label-count sequences matched in all 30 pairs, placing the intervention downstream. The combined change does not distinguish stronger regularization, lower capacity and removal of sampling. [Formal report](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/classifier-stage-a-report.md) · [Mechanism analysis](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/classifier_lowinput/mechanism.md).

These results locate useful intervention points without identifying one universal cause. Broader calibration can rescue some weak starts; broader self-training and a simpler model can also damage valid identifications. Large run-to-run changes arise from interactions between calibration evidence, learned ranking, candidate selection and confidence thresholds. Changing a training threshold is different from relaxing the final reporting threshold: **all four experiments retained final q≤1%**.

## Scope, verification and decision

The [frozen prospective plan](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/PLAN.md) specified six low-input files at seeds 0, 1, 2, 5, 8 per arm. Every arm completed all 30 even after a scientific rejection became inevitable. The screen required fewer empty results or at least 10% lower relative reported-count range, plus no newly empty results, no greater than 5% per-file median loss at either entrapment threshold or in reported human count, and no more than 0.5 percentage-point increase in pooled FDP. None passed all criteria. No control panels, reserved-seed confirmation or additional-file confirmation were launched, as specified for failed screens. No candidate was retuned or combined after its results were seen.

Core required-data tests passed for each prototype: evidence 29, RT 31, decoys 29 and classifier 25; release builds and CLI smoke checks passed. Tests explicitly required the real fixtures so missing-data early returns could not masquerade as integration coverage. Frozen sources, protocols, patches, executables and validation logs remain in their candidate archives. Report calculation/provenance checks passed 17 tests; an independent final audit recomputed all five gates per arm and verified 1,146 unique artifact hashes, all nine frozen-plan entries, and identical V3 baseline metric rows and output bytes across arms. [Independent audit](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/independent-final-audit.json).

[Worktree provenance](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/worktrees.json) records four actual Git worktrees of a private V3 snapshot, preserving the main repository's pre-existing uncommitted changes. A [default-preservation audit](../benchmark/results/stability_round2/default-preservation-audit.json) confirms 35 main-source files and 168 external files remain unchanged; the default executable remains SHA256 `50b4227bca68a58283368d7189dd65dbbd54854f340d05d2ca355824c4ad1738`.

These are one bounded representative per proposed approach, including compound changes within some arms. The multiple-decoy training heuristic is not a validated protein-level averaged target–decoy FDR procedure. The classifier experiment does not test externally pretrained or shared models. Seed reruns of fixed files are correlated and cannot establish general biological robustness. Rounded trace agreement does not prove equality of all internal floating-point values or training identities. Per-file sample CVs, individual FDPs, every loss, core/stress summaries and accepted-list overlap are retained in the formal JSON reports.

**Decision: keep accepted V3.** RT provides the clearest evidence of a useful direction, but this global replacement is not sufficiently reliable to adopt.
